After your visit

What's normal afterwards.

Most after-effects settle within a day or two on their own. Here is what to expect, and what is worth a message instead of waiting.

Numbness fades in 2 to 4 hours

Hold off on hot drinks and chewing until the feeling is fully back, so you do not bite your cheek by accident.

Mild tenderness is expected

Teeth can feel bruised for a day or two after a filling or root canal. Ordinary pain relief is usually enough.

Easy on the hard stuff

Skip very hot, crunchy or sticky food for the rest of the day, and avoid straws for a few days after an extraction.

Message us if something is off

Growing swelling, a fever, bleeding that will not stop, or pain getting worse after day three. Send a message and describe it.
