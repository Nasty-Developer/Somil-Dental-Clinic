General consultation

A short examination and an honest conversation about what you actually need, without booking any treatment. Useful when something feels wrong but you cannot name it, or when you want a second opinion before deciding.

Benefits

Why patients choose it

  • Find out what is going on before committing to treatment
  • Know the options and the cost of each
  • Second opinions welcome, with your existing reports
  • No pressure to book anything on the day
Procedure overview

What happens, step by step

  1. You describe the problem in your own words, with any reports or X-rays you already have.
  2. The mouth is examined and the findings explained in plain language.
  3. Options, timelines and costs are laid out, including doing nothing for now.
  4. You decide what, if anything, to book, and when.
Duration

How long it takes

Booked as a 20-minute appointment. About 20 minutes in practice; the exact plan is confirmed after the examination.

Afterwards

What to expect later

Nothing to recover from. You leave with the findings, the options and the costs in writing.

FAQ

Questions patients ask

What is a general consultation?
A conversation and examination without committing to treatment: what is happening, what the options are, what each costs. It is the right appointment if you are unsure what you need.
How long does it take?
About 20 minutes, plus X-rays if they are needed.
Can I bring my reports or X-rays?
Yes. Bring anything you already have. It often saves a repeat X-ray and makes the plan more accurate.
