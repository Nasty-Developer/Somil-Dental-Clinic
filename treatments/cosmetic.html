Cosmetic dentistry

Benefits

Why patients choose it

  • Chipped, worn or uneven front teeth
  • Gaps or a smile line you have never been happy with
  • Old, discoloured fillings that show when you smile
  • Teeth that look out of proportion with your face
Procedure overview

What happens, step by step

  1. We photograph and plan the change, then agree the shape and shade with you.
  2. Minimal tooth structure is prepared, or composite is built up directly on the tooth.
  3. The result is fitted, checked for bite, and polished.
Duration

How long it takes

Booked as a 45-minute appointment. Veneers & bonding in practice; the exact plan is confirmed after the examination.

Afterwards

What to expect later

Bonding can feel slightly rough for a day. Veneers may feel sensitive to cold for a short while.

FAQ

Questions patients ask

Will people be able to tell I had work done?
The aim is a result that looks like your teeth, only better proportioned. Shapes and shades are agreed with you before anything is prepared.
How much tooth is removed for veneers?
As little as possible, and sometimes none at all for composite bonding. The plan always states what is being prepared and why.
How long do veneers last?
With good care, 10 years or more. Bonding is more affordable but needs refreshing after a few years.
