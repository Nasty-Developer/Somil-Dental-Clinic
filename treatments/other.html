Benefits

Why patients choose it

  • Fillings, crowns and bridges to repair damaged teeth
  • Dentures and gum treatment when more is needed
  • Night guards and sports guards to protect the teeth
  • One place for the small things that do not need their own page
Procedure overview

What happens, step by step

  1. Tell the clinic what is bothering you, or what has been advised elsewhere.
  2. The tooth or area is examined, with an X-ray where it adds information.
  3. You get the options, the alternatives and the cost of each before anything begins.
  4. Treatment is carried out and reviewed, with aftercare explained at the chair.
Duration

How long it takes

Booked as a 30-minute appointment. Fillings to dentures in practice; the exact plan is confirmed after the examination.

Afterwards

What to expect later

Depends on the treatment carried out. Anything that needs specific aftercare is explained at the chair and written down for you.

FAQ

Questions patients ask

What counts as an other dental treatment?
Fillings, crowns and bridges, dentures, gum treatment, wisdom tooth assessment, night guards, sports guards, sensitivity treatment and emergency repairs. If it is not listed on this page, ask and it is probably still something the clinic does.
How do I know which treatment I need?
You do not have to. Book this option, describe the problem in the message, and the clinic will advise after examining you.
Can two treatments be done in one visit?
Often yes, if they are on the same side and the visit has enough time. Say so in your booking message and it will be planned in.
