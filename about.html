Consultation

Your first visit, in four steps.

Thirty to forty minutes, most of it listening. Nothing is done on the first visit unless it is urgent and you decide to go ahead with it.

STEP 01Talk

What is bothering you, how long it has been going on, and what you have already tried. This part takes longer than you would expect.

STEP 02Look

Teeth, gums, bite and old work are examined. X-rays are taken only where they change the answer.

STEP 03Explain

What we can see, what the options are, and the trade-offs of each, including doing nothing for now.

STEP 04Plan

A written plan with costs before anything starts. You take it away and decide in your own time.

How to come prepared

  • Recent X-rays or reports from another clinic, if you have them
  • A list of medicines you take, including blood pressure tablets and blood thinners
  • Your insurance card, if you plan to use it
  • Your questions, written down, so none of them get forgotten

Questions worth asking

  • Do I need this now, or can it wait six months?
  • What are the alternatives, including doing nothing?
  • What does the full cost include, and what might be added later?
  • How many visits will this take, and how far apart?
